# StarBar 1.11.0 — Stars with a flourish, and Spotify on other devices left alone

StarBar 1.11.0 plays a flourish over the stars when you rate a song, with a finale for five stars, keeps Spotify songs playing on another device off your screen, and gets the cover of an Apple Music song onto the strip before it slides up.

**This is the recommended release. Requires macOS 13 or later, and Music or Spotify.**

## Stars with a flourish

- Rate a song in StarBar and the stars pop one after another from the left, the last one hardest. A star the new rating lights grows out of its dot as the run reaches it.
- Give a song five stars and each star throws a small star in one of StarBar's five colours as the run passes it. They fan out below the menu bar, spinning as they go, and fade away. Five stars chosen again for a song that already had them only pop.
- Lowering or removing a rating is quieter: the stars fold back into their dots from the right.
- While you hold the button down and drag across the stars, each one grows or shrinks as you pass it, rather than switching on and off.
- If the Music Video strip is up for the song you rate, its stars play the same flourish, with the small stars flying up over the strip, and the strip waits for them before it slides away.
- Only your own ratings set it off, by click, drag or shortcut. A rating changed in Music, or a new song that already has one, shows its stars without a flourish.
- With Reduce Motion on, the stars cross-fade instead, with no finale.

## Spotify on other devices

- When Spotify plays on another device through Spotify Connect, such as a speaker in another room, the Spotify app on your Mac still announces every song. StarBar now tells the two apart, and keeps songs playing elsewhere off the Music Video strip and out of the menu bar, which stays with Music.
- Those songs aren't scrobbled to Last.fm either. If you move a song from your Mac to a speaker, only the time it played on your Mac counts towards scrobbling it.
- To show them anyway, check “Show Spotify songs playing on other devices” under Spotify in the Accounts tab of Preferences. It's off by default, and it needs no Spotify sign-in.
- StarBar tells where Spotify is playing by whether Spotify is sending sound to your Mac's speakers or headphones, which macOS reports from macOS 14.2. On earlier versions every Spotify song shows as before, and the checkbox is ticked and greyed out.
- A song you skip to on your Mac while the last one is still playing reaches the strip and the menu bar about one and a half seconds late, while StarBar checks that the sound is still coming from your Mac. Spotify announces a song started on a speaker a moment before your Mac's sound stops, so without the check that song would show.

## Apple Music covers, on time

- For an Apple Music song that isn't in your library, the cover is now usually on the strip as it slides up, where before the strip often showed a grey placeholder and the cover faded in over it. StarBar asks Apple for the cover as soon as Music says the song has started, instead of once the strip is ready.
- The first such song after StarBar opens can still show the placeholder for a moment, while the first connection to Apple is made.
- A cover that arrives before its place on the strip is on screen appears at once, with no fade.

## Network access

The request to Apple for covers is the one StarBar 1.9.0 added, made earlier. With the Music Video strip on, StarBar sends it as soon as Music says a song from the Apple Music catalogue has started, before it has asked Music whether the song is in your library. So it is now also sent for songs you own when Music plays them from the Apple Music catalogue rather than from your library, as it can when you play an album from its page in Apple Music. What is sent is unchanged: the song's artist and title, and your Mac's region, which picks the store to search, to Apple's iTunes Search service at `itunes.apple.com`, then a download of the cover from `mzstatic.com`. Songs played from your library still never cause it, and nothing else is new. The [privacy policy](https://github.com/rossshannon/StarBar/blob/main/Privacy%20Policy.md) is updated to say so.

## Privacy

Telling where Spotify is playing takes one new thing read on your Mac: whether the Spotify app is sending sound to an output, which macOS reports without asking you. StarBar reads only that, never the sound itself, and keeps none of it.

## Installation

Download the zip, extract StarBar and move it to Applications. Allow StarBar to control Music, and Spotify if you use it, when prompted.

This build is signed ad hoc and is not notarised. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. A SHA-256 checksum accompanies the zip.
