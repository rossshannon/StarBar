# StarBar 1.8.0 — Music ratings for Spotify songs you own

StarBar 1.8.0 shows your Music ratings for the Spotify songs you already own. When Spotify plays a song that's in your Music library, the menu bar and the announcement strip show that song's stars and favourite heart, and a rating you choose is saved to it in Music.

**This is the recommended release. Requires macOS 13 or later, and Music or Spotify.**

## Spotify songs you have in Music

- When Spotify plays a song that's also in your Music library, the menu bar shows its Music stars and heart where Spotify's icon and green heart would be. The announcement strip shows them too.
- Click or drag across the stars, or use the rating shortcuts, and the rating is saved to the song in Music. The heart sets or clears its favourite in Music.
- For these songs the heart is Music's favourite, not Spotify's like. To like one of them in Spotify, use Spotify itself. Songs you don't have in Music show Spotify's icon and heart, as in 1.7.
- Music has to be open, though it can stay in the background. StarBar never opens Music for this. With Music closed, Spotify songs show as they did in 1.7.
- StarBar matches only the same recording. A live version, remix, acoustic take, demo or re-recording matches only that same version, so a live track in Spotify never puts a rating on your studio copy. Edition words such as "Remastered 2011" or "Radio Edit", and featured artists, don't stop a match. When StarBar can't tell, it shows Spotify's heart rather than guess.
- If you have the song more than once, StarBar picks the copy on the album Spotify is playing from, then one you've rated, then the closest in length, then the one you've played most.
- The first Spotify song after StarBar starts takes a second or two to show its stars, while StarBar reads the names in your library. After that the stars show within about a quarter of a second.
- A few songs are missed. A song credited in Music to "Diana Ross & The Supremes" isn't found for a Spotify song credited to The Supremes alone, because a one-word name after the first is as often a backing band.
- A rating can't be undone in Music, so StarBar logs every rating it saves from Spotify, with both songs and the rating it replaced. Look in Console for the StarBar process.

## Privacy

Nothing new goes over the network. Matching happens on your Mac: StarBar asks Music for the titles and artists in your library, the same way it already reads and rates the playing song, and keeps them in memory only. It reads them again only after Music says the library changed, and only when the next Spotify song needs them. It never checks the library on a timer.

## Fixed

- A rating or heart you've just chosen no longer flicks back to the old one while Music is still saving it, or when Music is slow to answer.
- When Music is slow to answer, for example after sitting idle, the stars on show stay as they are rather than dropping to unrated.
- Letting go of a drag across the stars after the song has changed no longer rates the new song.
- The reminder to rate a song no longer rings for a song you've rated when Music is slow to answer.

## Installation

Download the zip, extract StarBar and move it to Applications. Allow StarBar to control Music, and Spotify if you use it, when prompted.

This build is signed ad hoc and is not notarised. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. A SHA-256 checksum accompanies the zip.
