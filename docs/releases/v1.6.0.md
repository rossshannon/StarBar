# StarBar 1.6.0 — Spotify

StarBar 1.6.0 follows Spotify as well as Music: the announcement strip slides up for new Spotify songs and podcast episodes, and the menu bar shows a green heart while Spotify is playing.

**This is the recommended release. Requires macOS 13 or later, and Music or Spotify.**

## Spotify

- When a new song or podcast episode starts in Spotify, the announcement strip slides up with its title, artist, album and cover art. Its bottom row says "Spotify" where Music's stars would be. Adverts are never announced.
- While Spotify is playing, the menu bar shows a green heart. Clicking it brings Spotify to the front.
- With Music and Spotify both open, StarBar follows whichever one is actually playing. Pausing one and playing the other hands over straight away, and a song you resume isn't announced a second time.
- The first time a Spotify song needs its artwork, macOS asks whether StarBar may control Spotify. StarBar uses that only to ask Spotify for the artwork's address.
- Spotify sends nothing when a song goes back to its start, so a restarted Spotify song isn't announced again. Music songs still are.
- Liking songs in Spotify isn't supported yet. Spotify's scripting can't do it; it's planned for 1.7 through Spotify's own sign-in.

## Network access

StarBar now makes one kind of network request: it downloads the cover art of the Spotify song that's playing, from Spotify's image server (`i.scdn.co`). Nothing else goes over the network. The only thing the request reveals is which cover is wanted, and it goes to Spotify, which already knows what you're playing. If you don't use Spotify, StarBar makes no network requests at all.

## About and Preferences

- The About window is redesigned around the app icon, with the licence in a sheet that opens at the top.
- Preferences drops the labels beside its checkboxes, which only repeated what the checkboxes say. The announcement checkbox now reads "Show a Music Video strip when a new song plays".

## Installation

Download the zip, extract StarBar and move it to Applications. Allow StarBar to control Music, and Spotify if you use it, when prompted.

This build is signed ad hoc and is not notarised. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. A SHA-256 checksum accompanies the zip.
