# StarBar 1.7.1

StarBar 1.7.1 puts Spotify's icon in the menu bar, so you can always get to Spotify with one click, and shows the cover in the popover for songs you open in Apple Music.

**This is the recommended release. Requires macOS 13 or later, and Music or Spotify.**

## Spotify

- While a Spotify song is playing, the menu bar shows Spotify's icon to the left of the green heart. Click the icon to bring Spotify to the front. If you had closed Spotify's window, it opens again.
- The heart works as before: once you've connected your Spotify account in Preferences, clicking it likes or unlikes the song. Without connecting, it brings Spotify to the front too.
- The icon follows Spotify's brand guidelines. It is drawn in one colour, so it matches the rest of the menu bar in light and dark mode.

## Fixed

- The popover now shows the cover for a song you play from its Apple Music album page, even when the song is in your library. It reads the cover from your own copy of the song, as the announcement strip already did, rather than from the Apple Music copy that's playing, which sometimes reports no artwork.

## Installation

Download the zip, extract StarBar and move it to Applications. Allow StarBar to control Music, and Spotify if you use it, when prompted.

This build is signed ad hoc and is not notarised. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. A SHA-256 checksum accompanies the zip.
